Juventus Not Involved in Race to Sign Brescia's Sandro Tonali
Aug 11, 2020

Juventus are not interested in pursuing a deal to sign Brescia's Sandro Tonali, despite previous reports linking them with a move for the highly-rated youngster. The midfielder is continually being linked with a big move this summer as clubs at the top of Serie A look set to fight for his signature. Juve's 'interest' isn't to be, however, according to Goal's Juventus correspondent Romeo Agresti. He confirmed the news in a tweet, quashing their interest despite seemingly being keen on breathing new life into their midfield.
