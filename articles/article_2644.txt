Samuel Umtiti: Barcelona confirm loan move to Lecce
Aug 25, 2022
https://www.90min.com/posts/samuel-umtiti-barcelona-loan-lecce
Barcelona have confirmed defender Samuel Umtiti has joined Italian side Lecce on loan for the 2022/23 season. Once one of the best centre backs in the world and still only 28 years of age, the Frenchman started confidently at Camp Nou following his move from Lyon in 2016 and was soon an important member of a Barca side that would win two La Liga titles. However, the World Cup winner consistently struggled with injuries, with a knee problem being particularly damaging, and he has since been usurped in central defence by the likes of Ronald Araujo, Eric Garcia, Andreas Christensen and Jules Kounde.
