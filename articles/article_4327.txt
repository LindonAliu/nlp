Contract talks between Bayern Munich and Kingsley Coman have broken down and the winger could now be sold in the transfer window. The Frenchman has been holding discussions with the Bundesliga champions over an contract, with the 24-year-old's current deal due to expire in the summer of 2023. However, according to BILD, the latest round of talks ended several weeks ago and the involved parties are no closer to reaching an agreement. Marco Neppe, Bayern's technical director, is leading the negotiations on behalf of the club and is said to be frustrated by Coman's financial demands.
