Paul Pogba has shared a video from on board a private jet to tease his imminent return to Juventus. The midfielder left Manchester United for the second time at the end of his contract and is set to sign for the Bianconeri - also for a second time - on a free transfer after the Italian giants saw off competition from the likes of Real Madrid and Paris Saint-Germain. 90min revealed on Tuesday that Pogba was now on the verge of a move back to Turin having agreed on a four-year contract at Allianz Stadium back in June, with the 29-year-old set to undergo a medical this week. The player himself has now all but confirmed that an announcement is incoming. Taking to social media, Pogba posted a video of himself sitting on a private jet, looking pretty happy. "It's time...it's time...yes sir!" he says, before adding in Italian: "See you soon."
