Borussia Dortmund sign Anthony Modeste on one-year deal
Aug 8, 2022
https://www.90min.com/posts/borussia-dortmund-sign-anthony-modeste-on-one-year-deal
Borussia Dortmund have confirmed the signing of Koln striker Anthony Modeste on a one-year contract. BVB recruited Sebastian Haller from Ajax to replace Erling Haaland, but the Ivory Coast international later discovered a testicular tumour during a pre-season training camp in Switzerland.  Modeste is a proven Bundesliga striker and has enjoyed excellent campaigns with both Koln and Hoffenheim, most recently netting 20 league goals last season. BVB sporting director Sebastian Kehl said: ''After our analysis of last season, we made a conscious decision to recruit a classic centre-forward who could bolster our offensive game by offering physicality, a strong aerial presence and a goal threat.
