Wout Weghorst to undergo Man Utd medical

Besiktas have given Wout Weghorst permission to fly to England in order to finalise a move to Manchester United, sources have told 90min. The Red Devils recently identified the Netherlands target man as a short-term solution to their striker woes and made contact with parent club Burnley about taking over his loan deal from Besiktas. And Weghorst's new loan could be confirmed soon as 90min understands he is set to undergo a medical with United. While the deal is now progressing smoothly, United were initially not happy that they would have to wait for Besiktas to sort out a replacement for Weghorst, though were understanding of their position. But the Turkish giants have now agreed to let Weghorst leave before a replacement has signed on the dotted line.
