Mar 29, 2023

Leeds United sporting director Victor Orta has confirmed the club's interest in landing in-demand Barcelona teenager Ilias Akhomach. The teenager, who turns 19 in April, is out-of-contract this summer and Barca have accepted he will be leaving Camp Nou. Barca will allow him to depart on a free, but will insist on a percentage of any future profits from the club he joins. Leeds are very keen on the right winger, but they not alone with their interest, and 90min understands that Arsenal, Brentford, Brighton and Wolves also have an interested in bringing him to England. Sevilla, Monaco and AC Milan are also keen on the young Catalan, but Leeds are hopeful that Akhomach will opt for Elland Road.
