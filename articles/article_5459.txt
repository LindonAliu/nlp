Benfica Take Barcelona Defender Jean-Clair Todibo on Two-Year Loan
Oct 5, 2020
https://www.90min.com/posts/benfica-barcelona-defender-jean-clair-todibo-two-year-loan
Barcelona have reached an agreement with Benfica to allow defender Jean-Clair Todibo to join the Portuguese outfit on a two-year loan deal with an option to buy.  The Frenchman spent the second half of the 2019/20 season on loan at Schalke, and had been linked with a permanent move to Fulham during the 2020 transfer window. LATEST NEWS! Agreement with Benfica for loan of @jctodibo However, Barcelona announced on their official club website that they had agreed a deal with Benfica to take Todibo on loan.
